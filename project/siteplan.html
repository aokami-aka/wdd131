Mythos Atlas

WDD 131 - Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Mythos Atlas

The name "Mythos Atlas" represents a guide to the myths, gods, heroes, creatures, and stories found in different cultures around the world. "Mythos" refers to traditional stories and mythology, while "Atlas" represents a collection or guide that helps visitors explore different mythological traditions.

The name was chosen because it is short, memorable, and gives the website a sense of exploration and discovery.

Possible domain: mythosatlas.com

Logo concept: A stylized ancient compass or globe combined with a mythological symbol, representing the exploration of mythology from different cultures.

2. Site Purpose

Mythos Atlas will be an informational website about mythology from different cultures around the world. The website will introduce visitors to major mythological traditions and provide information about their gods, heroes, creatures, legends, and famous stories.

The site will initially focus on several traditions, such as Greek, Norse, Egyptian, and Japanese mythology. Visitors will be able to explore the different cultures and learn about important figures and stories from each tradition.

JavaScript will be used to provide interactive features, such as filtering mythology by culture or category and dynamically displaying additional information.

3. Scenarios

Potential visitors may ask questions such as:

  • What are the main gods and important figures in Norse mythology?
  • What is the difference between Greek and Egyptian mythology?
  • Which mythological creatures appear in Japanese mythology?
  • Where can I learn about famous myths from different cultures?

4. Color Schema

The color scheme is inspired by ancient manuscripts, mythology, and historical artifacts. Dark colors provide a mysterious atmosphere while gold is used as an accent to represent ancient treasures and mythology.

#101827
#C9A227
#F5EFE1

Dark Navy - #101827

Used for the header, footer, navigation, and major background elements. It provides contrast and creates a mysterious atmosphere.

Ancient Gold - #C9A227

Used for headings, borders, buttons, icons, and other accent elements. It represents ancient artifacts and mythology.

Warm Cream - #F5EFE1

Used as the main page background. It provides a warm appearance inspired by old manuscripts and parchment.

5. Typography

Cinzel

Cinzel will be used for the website title, main headings, and important section headings. Its classical appearance fits the historical and mythological theme of the website.

Merriweather

Merriweather will be used for paragraphs and general body text. It provides good readability while maintaining a traditional appearance.
